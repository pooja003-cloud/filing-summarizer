# Top 5 risks: Dell Technologies Inc. (DELL)

Source: Form 10-K, fiscal year ended 2026-01-30, accession 0001571996-26-000008, Item 1A Risk Factors. Run 3.

Quotes are unverified until check.py has run.

## 1. AI revenue concentrated in few large customers, straining margins and cash

AI servers drive growth, but buyers are few and orders are lumpy, so revenue and margins can swing sharply between periods. These large deals also tie up working capital and add credit risk from longer payment terms.

> to date our AI solutions have been purchased primarily by a small number of larger customers and cloud service providers. If we are not successful in continuing to expand sales to a broader base of customers, our ability to maintain growth in this area may be limited.

## 2. Dependence on vendors and concentrated contract manufacturers with minimal inventory

All components come from third parties and assembly is concentrated in a few contract manufacturers, mostly in Asia, while inventory buffers are thin. Shortages, tariffs or cost spikes could quickly hit shipments and gross margin.

> Because we generally maintain minimal levels of component and product inventories, a disruption in component or product availability could harm our ability to fill customer orders on a timely basis and at an acceptable price.

## 3. Large debt load reduces cash flow and financial flexibility

With approximately $31.5 billion of indebtedness, plus room to draw up to $5.0 billion of commercial paper and $5.9 billion under the revolver, debt service takes a large share of operating cash flow. The debt terms also allow significant additional borrowing, which matters for lenders' recovery and for equity holders' returns.

> As of January 30, 2026, we and our subsidiaries had approximately $31.5 billion of indebtedness.

## 4. Competition in ISG from cloud providers and a structurally weak PC market

ISG faces on-premises rivals and Infrastructure-as-a-Service providers, and will need more investment that could erode operating income. CSG, which absorbs overhead and provides scaled procurement, faces longer PC replacement cycles.

> ISG offers a portfolio of storage, server, and networking solutions, including AI-optimized technologies, and faces intense competition from existing on-premises competitors and increasing competitive pressures from Infrastructure-as-a-Service providers.

## 5. Controlling shareholders limit minority investor influence and governance protections

The MD and SLP stockholders hold approximately 91.7% of the total voting power. They can decide mergers, board elections and capital decisions that may not match the interests of Class C holders or creditors, and this may weigh on the Class C share price.

> the MD stockholders have the ability to approve any matter submitted to the vote of holders of all of the outstanding shares of the common stock voting together as a single class.
